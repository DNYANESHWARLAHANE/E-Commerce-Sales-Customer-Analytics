# Business Questions

## 1. Purpose of the Analysis

The purpose of this analysis is to convert e-commerce transaction data into business-focused insights that support understanding of sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, geographic performance, time-based patterns, and customer risk.

The following business questions were defined for the project.

---

## 2. Sales Performance Questions

### Q1. What is the overall revenue generated?

Determine the total revenue generated across the available transaction period.

### Q2. How many orders were placed?

Calculate the total number of unique orders/invoices.

### Q3. How many units were sold?

Measure the total quantity of products sold.

### Q4. What is the Average Order Value?

Calculate the average revenue generated per order.

### Q5. How does revenue change over time?

Analyze monthly revenue trends to identify changes in sales performance across the available transaction period.

---

## 3. Product Performance Questions

### Q6. Which products generate the highest revenue?

Identify the products contributing the most revenue.

### Q7. Which products have the highest sales volume?

Identify products with the highest number of units sold.

### Q8. Which products contribute significantly to overall sales?

Analyze product-level revenue contribution to understand sales concentration.

### Q9. Which products and sales activities contribute most to overall product performance?

Analyze product-level revenue, units sold, order frequency, and other available product performance measures.

---

## 4. Customer Analysis Questions

### Q10. How many identified customers are present?

Determine the number of customers available for customer-level analysis.

### Q11. Which customers generate the most revenue?

Identify high-revenue customers and understand customer revenue concentration.

### Q12. Which customers are repeat customers?

Analyze customer purchasing frequency to distinguish repeat customers from one-time customers.

### Q13. How is customer revenue distributed?

Examine customer value and revenue concentration across different customer groups.

### Q14. Which customers require additional attention?

Identify customer groups that may require additional attention based on their RFM characteristics.

---

## 5. RFM Analysis Questions

### Q15. What is the Recency of each customer?

Measure how recently each identified customer made a purchase.

### Q16. What is the Frequency of each customer?

Measure how frequently each identified customer placed orders.

### Q17. What is the Monetary value of each customer?

Measure the total revenue associated with each identified customer.

### Q18. How can customers be segmented using RFM?

Use Recency, Frequency, and Monetary scores to classify customers into meaningful business segments.

### Q19. How many customers belong to each RFM segment?

Measure the customer distribution across the defined customer segments.

### Q20. How does revenue contribution vary across customer segments?

Compare revenue contribution across the defined customer segments.

---

## 6. Customer Risk Questions

### Q21. How many customers are classified as At Risk?

Identify the number of customers belonging to the At Risk segment.

### Q22. How much revenue is associated with At Risk customers?

Calculate the revenue associated with customers classified as At Risk.

### Q23. Which high-value customers are at risk?

Identify customers with relatively high customer value who are also classified within the At Risk population.

### Q24. How is at-risk revenue distributed by customer value?

Analyze the distribution of At Risk customers and their associated revenue across the defined customer-value categories.

---

## 7. Geographic Analysis Questions

### Q25. Which countries generate the most revenue?

Compare revenue contribution across countries.

### Q26. How is revenue distributed geographically?

Analyze each country's contribution to overall sales.

### Q27. How many countries are represented in the transaction data?

Determine the geographic coverage of the dataset.

---

## 8. Time-Based Analysis Questions

### Q28. Which months generate the highest revenue?

Compare monthly revenue across the available transaction period.

### Q29. Does sales performance vary by year?

Compare monthly sales patterns across different years.

### Q30. At what hours is sales activity highest?

Analyze revenue, orders, and units sold by hour of the day.

### Q31. Which days of the week show higher sales activity?

Analyze revenue, orders, and units sold by day of the week.

---

## 9. Dashboard Questions

The Power BI dashboard was designed to provide interactive answers to the following broader questions:

- How is the business performing overall?
- Which customer segments are significant in terms of customer count and revenue?
- Where is customer revenue concentrated?
- Which customers may require additional attention?
- Which products perform strongly?
- Which countries contribute the most revenue?
- How does sales performance change over time?
- What patterns are visible across days and hours?

---

## 10. Analytical Objective

The overall analytical objective is to connect transaction-level data with customer, product, geographic, and time-based analysis to create a structured view of e-commerce business performance.

The analysis is intended to support data-driven understanding of:

**Sales → Customers → Products → Risk → Geography → Time**
