# RFM Methodology

## 1. Overview

RFM analysis was used to evaluate customer behavior and identify meaningful customer segments based on purchasing activity.

RFM stands for:

- Recency
- Frequency
- Monetary

The analysis was performed at the customer level using transactions with valid customer identification.

---

## 2. RFM Dimensions

### 2.1 Recency

Recency measures how recently a customer made a purchase.

It is calculated as the number of days between the customer's most recent purchase date and the analysis reference date.

A lower number of recency days indicates a more recent purchase.

---

### 2.2 Frequency

Frequency measures how often a customer made purchases.

In this project, frequency is based on the number of distinct invoices/orders associated with each customer.

A higher frequency indicates that the customer has purchased more frequently.

---

### 2.3 Monetary

Monetary measures the total revenue generated by a customer.

It is calculated by summing transaction revenue for each identified customer.

A higher monetary value indicates greater customer revenue contribution.

---

## 3. RFM Scoring

Each RFM dimension was converted into a score from 1 to 5.

The scoring system divides customers into five groups using quantile-based scoring.

### Recency Scoring

Recency is interpreted differently from Frequency and Monetary.

For Recency:

- More recent customers receive higher scores.
- Customers with lower recency days receive higher scores.
- Customers with higher recency days receive lower scores.

Therefore:

**R5 = most recent customers**

**R1 = least recent customers**

---

### Frequency Scoring

Frequency uses a 1-to-5 scale:

- F1 = lowest purchase frequency
- F5 = highest purchase frequency

Higher frequency receives a higher score.

---

### Monetary Scoring

Monetary uses a 1-to-5 scale:

- M1 = lowest customer revenue
- M5 = highest customer revenue

Higher monetary value receives a higher score.

---

## 4. RFM Score

The individual RFM scores are combined to create an RFM score.

The combined score is represented as:

```text
RFM Score = R Score + F Score + M Score

---

## 5. Customer Segmentation

The RFM scores were used to classify customers into behavioral segments.

The project uses customer-level RFM characteristics to identify different customer groups based on recency, purchase frequency, and monetary value.

The main customer segments used in the analysis are:

- Champions
- Loyal Customers
- Potential Loyalists
- At Risk
- Needs Attention
- Hibernating
- Big Spenders
- Recent Customers

These segments are used to support customer analysis in SQL, Python, and Power BI.

## 7. RFM Analysis Output

The final customer-level RFM dataset contains:

- 5,851 identified customers
- One RFM record per customer
- No duplicate customer IDs
- No missing customer IDs in the RFM dataset

The RFM output is used by the SQL analysis and Power BI dashboard for customer segmentation, customer-risk analysis, and revenue analysis.
