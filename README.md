## 📌 Project Overview

The **E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics** project analyzes transaction-level e-commerce data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, customer risk, geographic patterns, and time-based sales activity.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow:

```text
Raw Data
   ↓
Data Inspection
   ↓
Data Cleaning & Preparation
   ↓
Python Analytics
   ↓
SQL Business Analysis
   ↓
RFM Customer Segmentation
   ↓
Customer Risk Analysis
   ↓
Power BI Dashboard
   ↓
Business Insights
   ↓
Business Recommendations
