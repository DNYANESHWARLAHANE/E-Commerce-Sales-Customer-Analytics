# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## Portfolio Case Study

**Project Type:** End-to-End Data Analytics Project  
**Domain:** E-Commerce / Retail Analytics  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, MySQL, SQL, Power BI, Excel  
**Dataset:** UCI Online Retail II  
**Primary Focus:** Sales Performance, Customer Analytics, RFM Segmentation, Risk Analysis, Product Performance, Geography & Time

---

# 1. Project Overview

The **E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics** project is an end-to-end data analytics project designed to analyze retail transaction data and transform raw sales records into actionable business insights.

The project combines:

- Data quality inspection
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Sales performance analysis
- Product performance analysis
- Customer analytics
- RFM customer segmentation
- Customer risk analysis
- Geographic analysis
- Time-based sales analysis
- SQL-based business analysis
- Interactive Power BI dashboard development

The project follows a complete analytical workflow:

```text
Raw Retail Data
      ↓
Data Inspection
      ↓
Data Cleaning & Preparation
      ↓
Python Analytics
      ↓
SQL Business Analysis
      ↓
Customer RFM Analysis
      ↓
Power BI Dashboard
      ↓
Business Insights
      ↓
Business Recommendations
