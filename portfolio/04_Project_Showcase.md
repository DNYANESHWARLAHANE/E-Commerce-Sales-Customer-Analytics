# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## Project Showcase

A complete end-to-end data analytics project designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, customer behavior, customer value, customer risk, product performance, geography, and time-based sales patterns.

The project combines **Python, SQL, RFM Customer Segmentation, and Power BI** to transform raw transactional data into business-focused insights and decision-support recommendations.

# 2. Business Problem

An e-commerce business generates a large volume of transactional data containing information about orders, products, customers, countries, quantities, prices, and transaction dates.

Raw transactional data alone does not directly answer important business questions such as:

- How much revenue is being generated?
- How is sales performance changing over time?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which products sell the highest number of units?
- Which customers generate the most value?
- Which customers are loyal?
- Which customers may require attention?
- How much revenue is associated with at-risk customers?
- Which countries contribute most to revenue?
- At what times is sales activity highest?
- How can customer and sales analysis support business decisions?

The objective of this project is to convert the raw transaction data into structured analysis and actionable business insights.
